# Privacy Policy for Cozy Feelings

*Last updated: November 12, 2026*

Welcome to **Cozy Feelings** ("we," "our," or "the app"), developed by **Moon Flower**. Your privacy and trust are our top priorities. Cozy Feelings was designed from the ground up as a **privacy-first, local-first** daily mood and reflection journal.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Cozy Feelings mobile application.

---

## 1. Local-First Data Storage
* **All your journal entries, mood selections, notes, dates, tags, and settings are stored locally on your device** (using secure local device storage).
* We **do not** operate external tracking servers, analytics databases, or advertising networks.
* We **do not** collect, store, sell, monetize, or transmit your personal reflections or daily entries to any third party.

---

## 2. Google Drive Backup & Google User Data (Optional Feature)
Cozy Feelings offers an optional cloud backup and restore feature using Google Drive. This feature is completely voluntary and requires your explicit authorization.

### Scope of Access: `drive.appdata`
* Cozy Feelings requests only the **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata`** scope.
* This restricted scope gives the app access **only** to its own private, isolated Application Data folder on your Google Drive.
* **Cozy Feelings CANNOT and DOES NOT view, read, modify, or delete any of your personal files, Google Docs, photos, videos, or folders** on your Google Drive.
* The sole purpose of this permission is to allow you to save and restore your personal journal backup file (`cozy_feels_backup.json`).

### Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance
Cozy Feelings' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the **Limited Use** requirements:
* We do not transfer Google user data to third parties.
* We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or monetization.
* Human beings do not read your Google user data or backup files.

---

## 3. Account Disconnection and Data Deletion
* **Within the App:** You can disconnect your Google account at any time from the app's Backup settings.
* **From Google:** You can revoke Cozy Feelings' access to your Google account at any time by visiting your [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
* **Deleting Data:** You can delete individual entries directly in the app, reset app data, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored information.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy
Cozy Feelings does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

# Política de Privacidad de Cozy Feelings (Español)

*Última actualización: 12 de noviembre de 2026*

Bienvenido(a) a **Cozy Feelings** ("la aplicación"), desarrollada por **Moon Flower**. Tu privacidad y tranquilidad son nuestra máxima prioridad. Cozy Feelings fue diseñado desde el principio como un diario personal **privado y de almacenamiento local**.

### 1. Almacenamiento Local de Datos
* Todas tus entradas, reflexiones, estados de ánimo, etiquetas y configuraciones se guardan **única y exclusivamente de forma local en tu dispositivo**.
* No poseemos servidores externos de rastreo, analíticas ni publicidad.
* No recopilamos, vendemos ni compartimos tu información personal con terceros.

### 2. Copia de Seguridad con Google Drive (Opcional)
Cozy Feelings incluye una función opcional para crear y restaurar copias de seguridad en Google Drive.
* **Permiso utilizado (`drive.appdata`):** Solo solicita acceso a una carpeta oculta y privada propia de la aplicación en tu Google Drive.
* La aplicación **NO tiene acceso a tus fotos, documentos, carpetas ni archivos personales** de Google Drive. Solo guarda y lee su propio archivo de respaldo (`cozy_feels_backup.json`).
* Cumplimos estrictamente con la Política de Datos de Usuario de los Servicios de API de Google y los requisitos de Uso Limitado.
